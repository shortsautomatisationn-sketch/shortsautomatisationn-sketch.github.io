# Privacy Policy - Quiz Uploader

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Quiz Uploader is used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It requests the YouTube scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly solely to upload videos and read their status and statistics.

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data from Google user accounts is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising. No other users' data is collected.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To revoke access, visit https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
